# Campaign Overview

## The pitch
Three friends in the Forgotten Realms keep getting dragged into strange problems that turn out to be connected: a Feywild villain called The Hawk is collecting six Wonders that can rewrite reality. Each session so far was a one-off adventure, with The Hawk and the Wonders as the thread that carries forward.

## The party
Judy (elven fighter, Christmas elf, Katara), Dormir Amberforge (mountain dwarf Light cleric, Gabrielle) and Lyric Nightingale (halfling bard, Priscilla). Level 3. See `characters/`.

## How we got here
- **Session 1 (summer 2025):** Unstable unicorns, the Stable, Clarence and Princess Dicentra. The Hawk fled with the Horn. See `sessions/Session 1.md`.
- **Session 2 (Aug 1, 2026):** The North Pole, the reindeer problem, Noelle, The Hawk again. Santa kept the Star, Dormir got Starsilver, the party leveled to 3. See `sessions/Session 2.md`.

## Where things stand
The Hawk holds the Horn and the Vial. Santa keeps the Star. Three Wonders are still unnamed. See `Six Wonders.md` and `Plot Threads.md`.
