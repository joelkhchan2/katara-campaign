# Tone Guide

## Compass
About 50% mystery and clue-hunting, 30% comedy, 20% wonder. PG. Danger stays real but never grim. Land every eerie beat back in warmth. (Katara's love of strange nature may push wonder up; adjust here if the table agrees.)

## The table
- **Katara (13)** is mature, smart and quick-witted. Dry and sarcastic humor works. She likes puzzles, clues, and real strange nature facts. She is newer to the rules and less drawn to combat.
- **Gabrielle and Priscilla** are veterans playing for fun.
- Never talk down. Never script a PC.

## Content limits (PG)
- Fear and danger are fine. Gore, cruelty to animals, and graphic death are not.
- A villain's loss is implied (a name, a locket, an empty chair), never shown in detail.
- Romance stays light (Clarence and Dicentra is the ceiling).
- Humor punches at situations and bureaucracy, not at the players.

## What works here
- Clues she can hold and connect: a worksheet, a card, a list.
- Chaotic comedy built from characters (a horse in a trench coat, a CEO Santa, bureaucratic elves).
- Strange real nature as wonder: moths and light, the immortal jellyfish, tardigrades, deep-sea glow.
- An enemy who can be understood.

## Winks, not fixtures
Pusheen, Sanrio-style mascots, Unstable Unicorns, Avatar, Miraculous and Miniverse can show up as one small detail per scene. Never build a scene around a borrowed franchise.
