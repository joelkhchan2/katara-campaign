# Story Spine

The long arc, built to run from level 3 to about level 10 to 12. Estimated at 20 to 24 sessions, which at one session every 1 to 2 months is roughly 2 to 4 years. Level with milestones, about one level every two sessions. Level 10 is a natural stopping point if the schedule slips.

## The engine
The Hawk collects six Wonders, each granting a powerful spell. The party tries to stay ahead of him and learns why he wants them. Sessions stay mostly self-contained, each with a mystery and a Hawk or Wonder clue. Each PC's arc runs alongside (see `Plot Threads.md`).

## The six Wonders
See `Six Wonders.md`. The Horn (Wish), Star (Dream), Vial (Time Stop), Seed (True Polymorph), Hammer (Creation) and Song (Mass Suggestion).

## Chapters (levels and session numbers are estimates)
| Chapter | Levels | Sessions | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Loose Ends | 3 to 5 | 3 to 8 | One-off mysteries, each with a Hawk clue. The personal arcs begin. |
| 2. The Wonder Hunt | 5 to 7 | 9 to 14 | A race for the Hammer and the Song. The world widens beyond Waterdeep and the North Pole. |
| 3. The Hawk Unmasked | 8 to 10 | 15 to 19 | Who he was and what he lost. A Wonder falls. The personal arcs peak. |
| 4. The Last Wonder | 10 to 12 | 20 to 24 | The Seed, and the choice. |

## Endings (open, pick later)
1. He lets go: the party understands him and uses the last Wonder differently.
2. Partial: he undoes part of the loss at a cost the party has to live with.
3. The table decides: all six Wonders are present and the final choice is theirs.

## Rules to protect
- Danger stays real and the tone stays PG. The Hawk's loss is implied, never shown.
- The Wonders are a puzzle first. A 9th-level effect in a villain's hands is a reason the party cannot just fight him.
- Wonders can be used by anyone, including a PC. Most are once a day, and the Hammer creates material at any time. Decide the cost and limits before one is in a player's hands.
- Katara solves things herself with fair clues. Never script a PC's choices.
