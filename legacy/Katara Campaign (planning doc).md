Katara, apprentice detective at Zone of Sleuth, investigates the theft of a magical unicorn horn for her first mission. Clarence, who claims to be a unicorn, is actually a horse who found the horn and wants it back to become permanently magical. A mysterious villain, The Hawk, has been transforming unicorns into "unstable unicorns," corrupted minions led by the glamorous yet mischievous Queen Aurora Hooves. Katara must uncover the truth, defeat Queen Hooves and her unstable unicorn minions, confront The Hawk, and decide whether Clarence should receive his magical wish.

## Major Characters

* **Katara:** Judy the Christmas Elf, elven fighter.  
* **Gabrielle:** Dormir Amberforge, Dwarven cleric.  
* **Clarence (Horse/Unicorn)**: Horse pretending to be a unicorn; deeply desires to become permanently magical with his stolen horn.  
* **The Hawk (Mothkin)**: A mysterious Feywild villain inspired by Ladybug's Hawkmoth. He transforms creatures into unstable, chaotic minions to serve his goals.  
* **Princess Dicentra**: Charismatic yet troublesome unicorn queen who rules the unstable unicorns, enchanted and empowered by The Hawk. Her "Royal Hooves" grant her special powers.

**The Unicorn’s Missing Horn**

### Opening Scene: Mayhem in Baldur’s Gate

* The detectives begin in Baldur’s Gate marketplace. Suddenly, **unicorns rampage through the streets**—smashing carts, knocking barrels into the harbor, and frightening townsfolk.  
* These unicorns are *unstable*—their horns glow with strange Fey magic, their eyes shimmer with chaotic light.  
* A city watch captain shouts: *“Zone of Sleuth\! Handle this before it tears the city apart\!”*  
* Civilians to protect (skill challenge option: Persuasion DC12, Athletics DC12, etc., to help shepherd people away).

### Meeting Clarence

* Clarence approaches after the fight, disguised as a unicorn in a shabby trench coat.  
* He claims his **magical horn was stolen**, causing chaos in Baldur’s Gate and The Stable (Feywild unicorn court). Without it, the instability will spread.  
* Clarence offers them both trillby hats of disguise to look like unicorns and safe passage to The Stable demiplane.  
* **Hook**: Clarence promises **a Wish** if they recover the horn.  
  * *Katara*: This is her chance to prove herself and make her name at Zone of Sleuth.  
  * *Dormir*: The Wish could restore something important to her (let Gabrielle fill this in).

### Leads in Baldur’s Gate

1. **Arcana/Insight on Clarence**  
   * DC12: Clarence is heartbroken.  
   * DC15: Clarence is just a horse.  
     DC18: He wants the horn to become a real unicorn.  
2. **Witnesses** (talking to merchants, stablehands, or kids who saw unicorns) point toward a portal to *The Stable* demiplane.

### The Stable (Feywild Demiplane)

* Magical, colorful, but now unstable—flowers wilt, fountains bubble with rainbow sludge, and unicorns snarl at each other.  
  **Investigation DC12**: Princess Dicentra was once engaged to Clarence but broke it off after discovering his secret.  
* **DC15**: Dicentra has been isolating herself, hinting at betraying her council.  
  **DC18**: Dicentra’s sister, Iris, says she suspects Dicentra has allied with *The Hawk*, a mothkin who corrupts Fey creatures.

1. The unstable unicorns live in The Stable, which is their Fey Court. Nobody has seen their ruler, Princess Dicentra, and her attendants for days, with her palace locked to all outsiders.  
   1. Investigation (DC12):  
      1. Princess Dicentra was previously engaged to a unicorn from the Material Plane but that fell through after it was revealed he was just a horse.  
      2. The unicorns have been attacked by a mysterious force at night that they’ve called The Hawk because it swoops down and turns unicorns crazy.  
   2. Investigation (DC15):  
      1. They find and talk to a previous attendant who fled the palace after the unicorns inside went crazy. Apparently the Princess has been talking about getting rid of the council of unicorns who help her rule The Stable.  
      2. They had a misunderstanding: Princess Dicentra found out that Clarence is a horse and not a unicorn. He responded that “I guess we can’t be together” because he thought that’s why she was mad but she was only mad he lied.  
   3. Investigation (DC18):  
      1. They find the Princess’ sister Iris. The Princess was visited one night by a scary winged creature and Iris confronted her; she thinks the princess is in cahoots with The Hawk.  
      2. The Princess is planning to “make sure nobody can ever leave me again” by using the power of the Horn   
2. That night, the unicorns are turned unstable by a creature only known as The Hawk, who dives down and transforms them with his magic.  
   1. Unstable villager: HP11, AC12, Hooves \+3 to hit, 4 (1d6+1) damage.  
      1. Trampling Charge. If the horse moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a hooves attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the horse can make another attack with its hooves against it as a bonus action.  
3. The next morning, the Princess announces that she will be using the Horn to create a bubble around The Stable, preventing anyone from entering or leaving forever.  
   1. Unstable attendant: HP19, AC12, Hooves \+3 to hit, 7 (2d4+2) damage.  
      1. Trampling Charge. If the horse moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a hooves attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the horse can make another attack with its hooves against it as a bonus action.  
   2. Princess Dicentra: HP32, AC16, Hooves (2x) \+6 to hit, 7 (2d4+2) damage.  
      1. Royal Hooves: Command.  
      2. Unicorn Poison (doesn’t work on humans).  
      3. Royal Rumble: DC14 dex save or 1d8 damage \+ knocked prone.  
      4. Pack Tactics. The thug has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the thug's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

After freeing Princess Dicentra she says that she was heartbroken after Clarence left and that she would have accepted him even without the horn, which she casts away. Clarence comes out of hiding and asks if that’s true and they reunite, promising to have better communication going forward and ruling the kingdom together.

## Plot

* **First Case**: Apprentice detectives Judy and Dormir start their internship at Zone of Sleuth in Waterdeep.  
* **Clarence the Unicorn**: Clarence hires the detectives to recover his stolen magical unicorn horn, promising a Wish in return.  
  * **Secret Identity (DC15 Insight)**: Clarence is actually a horse who found a powerful unicorn horn artifact that temporarily transformed him.  
  * **Former Prince (DC18 Insight)**: Clarence is a royal horse who reveals he was set to marry Princess Dicentra but he had tricked her into believing he was a unicorn.  
* **The Stable**: Detectives travel to the Feywild, discovering unicorns corrupted into "unstable unicorns" causing whimsical chaos, ruled by Queen Dicentra.  
* **Princess Dicentra’s Court**: Dicentra took Clarence’s horn because she was angry he lied about being a unicorn.  
  * She reveals it was stolen again by her former ally, The Hawk.  
* **The Hawk’s Scheme**: The Hawk, a mothkin villain, corrupts Fey creatures (like Aurora and her unicorns) by amplifying negative emotions to harness chaotic magic.  
* **Final Confrontation**: Detectives defeat/redeem Dicentra, then confront The Hawk, reclaiming the horn and ending his chaotic spells.  
* **Resolution & Choice**: Detectives choose to return the horn to Clarence, keep it for themselves, or return it to Dicentra.
