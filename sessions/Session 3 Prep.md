# Session 3 Prep: Settling In

An interlude. The party gets a base, each PC gets a backstory beat, there is one short silly fight to refresh combat, and the session ends on an open Hawk clue. Estimated 3 to 4 hours.
Level: 3 (they levelled at the end of Session 2). Tone: mostly comedy and mystery, with a little wonder. PG, no evil or paranormal threats.

## Where things stand (DM only)
- Level 3. Judy, Dormir, Lyric.
- The Hawk holds the Horn and the Vial. Santa keeps the Star. Dormir has Starsilver.
- Noelle is in Christmas jail.
- Nobody knows what The Hawk plans next. Katara likes the Hawk story and wants to learn more.
- The party has around 10 gp and a few Session 2 items.

## Previously (read aloud, 4 sentences)
"Last time you went to the North Pole, where the reindeer had gone feral and Santa had gone missing. You found him in a bunker, but you did not catch the thing behind it, a moth-winged stranger called The Hawk. Dormir came away with a piece of the Star's silver. And everyone in Waterdeep now knows your names."

## Beats

### 1. Cold open: the keys (about 15 minutes)
A clerk from the city hands the party a ring of keys to a base of operations. Two versions are fully prepped: the Lantern House (`locations/The Lantern House.md`) and Thimble Cottage (`locations/Thimble Cottage.md`). The text below is written for the Lantern House. For the cottage, reskin the squatters (crows or squirrels in the rafters) and swap the moths for the cottage's own oddity (the extra door).

The clerk hands over keys to an old lighthouse on the cliffs. It is a reward for the North Pole business, or a bureaucratic mistake. Clerk: bored, deadpan, will not explain. Read-aloud:
"The lighthouse stands alone on the cliff, wind pulling at its door. Gulls complain from the rail. Inside, dust and old lamp oil, and 90 steps up a stairwell that has seen better days. Somewhere above you, something is rustling."
Hand it back: "What do you do?"
- The rustling is the squatters (Beat 4). Let them explore first.

### 2. Backstory beats (about 20 minutes each, questions only)
Ask these as open questions. Do not answer for the players.
- **Judy (Katara):** What does Judy notice in a lighthouse that only someone from the North Pole would? Why did her family leave? Who in the bookshop does she miss, if anyone?
- **Dormir (Gabrielle):** The first night, she sets up the forge. What is she making with the Starsilver, and who is it for?
- **Lyric (Priscilla):** How is her shop doing now, who helped rebuild, and what is the first song she plays in the new place?

### 3. The base (about 20 minutes)
They pick three upgrades together, paid from the raven hoard (Beat 4).
| Upgrade | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| A forge | 100 gp | A real forge for Dormir to work. No mechanical effect yet. |
| A reading room | 75 gp | A quiet room for research. One free hour of "study" per session on a clue. |
| A shop counter | 75 gp | Somewhere for Lyric to sell, and for the party to meet visitors. |
Total 250 gp. Leave about 50 gp. If they choose something else, price it in this range.

### 4. The squatters (a short silly fight, about 30 minutes)
Three ravens' nests' worth of birds have taken over the lamp room and hoarded shiny things: coins, spoons, a thimble, one very good lens cap. The birds are territorial and theatrical, not evil.
- **Setup:** Lamp room at the top of the stairs. The big lens and mirrors throw light everywhere. A gallery rail around the outside.
- **Creatures:** 3 swarms of ravens (stat block below). Aim for about 300 XP, an easy to medium fight for 3 level-3 PCs.
- **Ways through:**
  - **Fight:** Dormir's Radiance of the Dawn or Warding Flare in the lamp room, Judy's weapon attacks, Lyric's cantrips.
  - **Talk:** the ravens understand coins and trades. A shiny offering may buy a truce.
  - **Trick:** the lens focuses light. A mirror turned the right way startles the whole flock.
- **Make it sillier:** the birds steal an item from a PC mid-fight (a thimble, a hat). **Make it hotter:** a fourth swarm joins on round 3. **Shorter:** the flock bolts when two swarms are down.
- **The hoard:** about 300 gp of mixed coins and shiny junk. Items are worth less in the shop (the spoons are not silver).

**Swarm of Ravens** (SRD, verify against your books): AC 12, HP 24, speed 10 ft, fly 50 ft. Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing. Immune to charmed, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, prone, restrained, stunned. Passive Perception 15. **Beaks:** +4 to hit, reach 0 ft, one creature in the swarm's space, 7 (2d6) piercing, or 3 (1d6) if the swarm has half HP or fewer. CR 1/4.

### 5. Level-3 choices (about 15 minutes)
Offer short menus. The players choose.
- **Judy, Fighter subclass:** Champion (simple and strong), Battle Master (tricks and maneuvers), Eldritch Knight (a little magic).
- **Lyric, Bard college:** Lore (clever and knowledgeable) or Valor (fights alongside the party). Check what Priscilla wants.
- **Dormir:** new spells for level 3 (second-level slots). Ask Gabrielle.

### 6. Ending: the open Hawk clue (about 15 minutes)
At night the party watches the moths gather at the lamp. Do not explain them. Offer three observations the table may pick up on:
1. The moths do not circle the flame the way moths normally do. They line up, as if waiting.
2. The keeper's logbook on the shelf has pages torn out. The last entry reads only a date and "they came back."
3. On the lamp room floor, someone has drawn a rough ring of six small marks in the dust.

Do not say what any of this means. Ask: "What do you think it is?" Whatever the table guesses becomes a seed for Session 4. Keep a note of their theories in this file.

**One true Hawk fact for Katara (pick one, DM's choice):**
- He has a name the old Feywild courts used for him.
- He was seen carrying the Vial openly, as if it were something he needs.
- He did not take the Horn by force. It was given to him.
