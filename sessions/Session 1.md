# Session 1: Unstable Unicorns

**Played:** summer 2025 (exact date unknown). **Present:** Judy (Katara), Dormir (Gabrielle). **Level:** started at 2.
Source: Joel's original plan (`legacy/Katara Campaign (planning doc).md`), confirmed as "roughly followed" up to the princess battle. No session notes exist.

## What happened
- **Opening:** Unstable unicorns, their horns glowing with strange Fey magic, rampage through the Baldur's Gate marketplace. Judy and Dormir fight them off together and meet. Lyric's instrument shop is wrecked in the chaos.
- **Clarence:** A horse in a shabby trench coat claims to be a unicorn. He says his horn was stolen, which is causing chaos in Baldur's Gate and in The Stable (the Feywild unicorn court). He offers disguise trilby hats and safe passage to the Stable, and promises a Wish for the horn.
- **The Stable:** A Feywild demiplane gone wrong: wilting flowers, fountains of rainbow sludge, snarling unicorns. The party learns that Princess Dicentra was engaged to Clarence, discovered he is a horse, and broke it off because he lied. A winged creature, The Hawk, has been turning unicorns unstable at night.
- **The princess battle:** The party fights the unstable attendants and Princess Dicentra, who planned to use the Horn to seal the Stable forever. Freed from The Hawk's influence, she says she would have taken Clarence back without the horn, and they reunite.
- **Ending:** The Hawk flew off with the Horn.
- **Aftermath:** News spreads of "two unlikely heroes who saved people from a distant realm."
