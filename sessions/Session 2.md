# Session 2: The Reindeer Problem

**Played:** Aug 1, 2026. **Present:** Judy (Katara), Dormir (Gabrielle), Lyric Nightingale (Priscilla, new). **Level:** 2, levelled to 3 afterward.
Source: Gabrielle's notes (`legacy/Katara Campaign (Gabrielle session notes).md`) and Joel's confirmations. Joel's prep file (`legacy/Session 2 - Player Handouts.md`) is a plan only. Where prep and play differ, play wins.

## What happened
- **Setup:** Dormir has heard of Starsilver, a rare metal that appears only at certain times of year. Judy may know where it could be. The party is in Judy's bookshop in Waterdeep when Lyric comes in with a news article calling Judy and Dormir "two unlikely heroes." A reporter interviews them. Clarence arrives with a mission.
- **The mission:** The Hawk is back. Clarence is now a diplomat establishing kingdom relations. At the North Pole, the reindeer are going feral at night (foaming, wild eyes, attacking people) and the sleigh bells are silent. Mr. Claus, a CEO-style Santa all about efficiency, vanished days ago with the Star, which represents all wonder.
- **At the North Pole:** The party meets Bramble, the stable master, who has locked some reindeer up. The three VPs are Bramble, Fig (toy production, unlikeable, clear alibi) and Pib (keeper of the list and security, who may hold the key to the emergency bunker). Mrs. Claus feels overlooked.
- **Santa's office:** The pedestal is empty. Lyric spots a single Hawk feather and an unlatched window with a chair braced against it. There are scuff marks around the pedestal. A burnt note in the fireplace says not to put the Star on the sleigh.
- **The stables:** The reindeer go wild at night. Rudolph is immune.
- **The fight:** The party fights Noel(le) and The Hawk. Noelle is not redeemed. She goes to Christmas jail (assumed by Joel, not confirmed at the table).
- **The Hawk's speech:** He has wanted the Star for a long time. It is one of six Wonders. Together they can control reality, and the Horn lets its holder cast Wishes. The Star controls all wonder. He wants a power stronger than a Wish.
- **Ending:** The party rescues Santa. The Hawk flies off. The party does not keep the Star, and Dormir gets some Starsilver (the Star is made of it).
- **Loot:** 10 gp, 2 healing potions (2d4+2), 1 Nog Wild (2d4 temp HP plus a wild magic roll), 1 coal bomb (2d6 fire, DC 13 save for half), a cocoa thermos (5 temp HP, advantage on cold).
