# Clarence
A horse who pretended to be a unicorn after finding the Horn, because he wanted to be permanently magical. Engaged to Princess Dicentra until she found out. They reunited at the end of Session 1. Now a diplomat establishing relations between kingdoms; he brought the party Session 2's mission.
- **Want:** To keep Dicentra and to do right by her kingdom. Played.
- **Proposed (not played):** Voice tic: apologizes for things that are not his fault. Physical tic: tugs the collar of a trench coat that has never fit.
