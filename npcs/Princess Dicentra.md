# Princess Dicentra
Ruler of the Stable, the Feywild unicorn court. She broke off her engagement to Clarence when she learned he was a horse, then took the Horn, intending to seal the Stable forever. After the party freed her from The Hawk's influence, she said she would have taken Clarence back without the horn. They reunited.
- **Want:** Not to be left again. Played.
- Old docs also call her Queen Aurora Hooves. Use Dicentra.
