## Base comforts
A long rest at the party's base ends with a warm meal and a good bed. Each PC gains a d4 bless: add 1d4 to one attack roll, ability check or saving throw of their choice before the next long rest. Once used, it is gone. This applies to either base (the Lantern House or Thimble Cottage).
