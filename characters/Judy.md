# Judy

**Player:** Katara (13). New to the game: two sessions in two years before this campaign. **Class:** Elven fighter, level 3. **Subclass:** not chosen yet. No character sheet on file.

## Background
- A Christmas elf. Her family left the North Pole and runs a bookshop in Waterdeep. Session 2 opened in that shop.
- Met Dormir in Session 1 in the Baldur's Gate marketplace while fighting unstable unicorns. They are friends.
- Gabrielle's Session 2 notes say Judy may know where Starsilver could be found.

## Table notes
- Likes puzzles, mysteries and working out clues. Quick and sarcastic-friendly. Less drawn to combat.
- Give her something to hold or write on, a clue she can connect, and a moment where she can lead.

## Hooks for the DM (situations, never scripted choices)
- She grew up at the North Pole, so she may recognize things the others cannot. Offer this as a question ("Does this look right to you?"), not as a fact.
- Her family's bookshop is a base the party can return to, and a place where news and rumors turn up.
- Starsilver: she may know where it appears. Let her say.
- Fig, Pib and Bramble: she may know them or know of them.

## Open questions to ask Katara (never answer for her)
- Why did your family leave the North Pole?
- What did you think of the way Mr. Claus ran things?
- Which subclass appeals at level 3? Offer a short menu in plain terms: Champion (simple and strong), Battle Master (tricks and maneuvers), Eldritch Knight (a little magic). Let her pick, and do not push.
- Who in the bookshop does she like?
