# Lyric Nightingale

**Player:** Priscilla. Experienced. **Class:** Halfling bard, level 3. **Subclass:** unknown. Bard college is chosen at level 3, so it may be open. No character sheet on file. Joined in Session 2.

## Background
- Her instrument shop was destroyed in the Session 1 market incident. She had to get her instruments back to reopen.
- Read a news article about "two unlikely heroes" (Judy and Dormir) at the start of Session 2.
- Spotted the single Hawk feather in Santa's office.

## Table notes
Experienced player who plays for fun. She has not fleshed out Lyric beyond the notes. Offer hooks and ask, never assume.

## Hooks for the DM (situations, never scripted choices)
- Music and the North Pole: the silent sleigh bells, a reindeer that responds to a tune.
- The rebuilt shop: customers, rumors, a repair bill.
- The Hawk's feather and other small details she is good at noticing.
- A song that matters to someone in a place the party visits.

## Open questions to ask Priscilla
- Where did Lyric's instruments end up, and who helped her get them back?
- What is her family, and does anyone back home know about the shop?
- What kind of music does she play, and which instrument is her favorite?
- Which bard college, if she has decided?
