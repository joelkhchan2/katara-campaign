# Dormir Amberforge

**Player:** Gabrielle. Experienced. **Class:** Mountain dwarf cleric, Light domain, level 3. Source: `legacy/Dormir Cleric Lvl 2.pdf` (level 2, out of date).

## Sheet (level 2)
- **AC** 18 (breastplate and shield), **speed** 25. **Spell save DC** 13, **spell attack** +5.
- **Attacks:** battleaxe +3 (1d8+3), light crossbow.
- **Cantrips:** Light, Guidance, Sacred Flame, Word of Radiance.
- **Domain spells:** Burning Hands, Faerie Fire. **Prepared:** Cure Wounds, Guiding Bolt, Inflict Wounds.
- **Features:** Darkvision, Dwarven Resilience, Stonecunning, Dwarven Combat Training, tool proficiency, Channel Divinity (Radiance of the Dawn, once per rest), Warding Flare.
- **Level 3 update:** not recorded. Check with Gabrielle for new spells and slots.

## Personality (from the sheet)
- Can be rough but tries to be well intentioned.
- Protect at any cost.
- Flaw: expects others to be as clear and honest as she is, and cannot see the gray area.

## Background
- A mountain dwarf from the Amberspire Mountains. Her family forges the brightest metals. She is out adventuring to find the purest metal to bring home.
- Met Judy in Session 1. Judy's bookshop is where they meet between adventures.
- After Session 2 she has some Starsilver.

## Table notes
Experienced player who plays for fun. She has not fleshed out Dormir beyond the sheet. Offer hooks and ask, never assume.

## Hooks for the DM (situations, never scripted choices)
- Starsilver and where it comes from, and who might want it.
- Someone who lied to her. Her flaw makes her take dishonesty hard (compare Clarence and Dicentra).
- The Hawk's unstable victims, who are the kind of person she wants to protect.
- Her family: a letter, a messenger or a rumor from the Amberspire Mountains.
- The Light domain: a dark place that Light, Radiance of the Dawn or Warding Flare can solve.

## Open questions to ask Gabrielle
- Which deity does Dormir serve? The sheet does not say.
- What does her family forge, and what would they do with Starsilver?
- What did she wish for when Clarence offered a Wish in Session 1?
- What did she decide at level 3?
