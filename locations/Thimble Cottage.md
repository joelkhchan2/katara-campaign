# Thimble Cottage

**Status:** Proposed base (Session 3 option D, the cottage that is bigger inside). Working name only; the players can rename it. Not yet played.
**Where:** Wedged between a cobbler's and a chandler's on a crooked lane in Waterdeep's Dock Ward.
**One line:** A cottage the size of a garden shed from outside and a whole home inside.

## From outside
A perfect little house, no wider than a market stall. It has a round window with a flower box, a chimney the size of a teapot, and a door that comes to a halfling's shoulder. Anyone taller has to duck to get in. The brass bell-pull is a tiny key. Neighbors have stopped asking about it.

## The magic of the place
- **Bigger inside.** Cross the threshold and the cottage breathes out. The hall stretches, the ceiling lifts, and the whole interior is a full-sized home.
- **A kind house.** The cottage is fond of its residents. Doors adjust to the height of whoever opens them. Hooks lengthen. Chairs shift to fit. It never speaks and it never traps anyone.
- **Quiet from outside.** The noise inside never reaches the street, and the lane's noise barely reaches the cottage. A good place to hide, and a good place to host.
- **The extra door.** One day a door will appear that is not on the plan. See "Secret" below.

## Room by room
### The doorstep and the Hall of Coats
A narrow hall that widens as you walk. Hooks stretch to meet your hand. The floor tiles light softly under every step.
- *Read-aloud:* "You duck through a door meant for someone smaller. The hall opens like a held breath let go, and the ceiling lifts. A row of hooks reaches toward your coat."

### The Long Kitchen
A big wooden room with a black stove, a kettle that is always the right temperature, and a long table. In the morning the pantry holds simple, good food for three: bread, cheese, fruit, something warm. It is never fancy. The house just likes a full table.
- *Read-aloud:* "Warm bread and apples. The kettle is already on, and it does not whistle until you reach for it."
- **Accommodations:** stove, pump, pantry restocked each morning with plain food.

### The Great Room
A round room with a hearth, three armchairs that shape themselves to whoever sits, and a rug woven as a map of Waterdeep. The fire is always the right size. A mantel is lined with small objects from previous owners.
- *Read-aloud:* "The chair rearranges itself under you and decides it likes you. The fire pops once, politely."

### The Reading Loft
A mezzanine with ladders and rolling steps among tall shelves. A window seat looks over the garden. Some of the books are blank, as if waiting to be filled.
- *Read-aloud:* "The shelves go up and up, and a ladder rolls itself over before you ask. A few of the spines are empty, and a pen waits on the sill."
- **Opportunity:** a place for research and the case notebook.

### The Smithy
A small stone forge off the back garden, with a flue that runs up behind the house and out of a chimney too small for it. The chimney draws anyway.
- *Read-aloud:* "The forge is already warm. The bellows hang ready, as if someone had just stepped out. It smells of coal and clean iron."
- **Accommodations:** forge, bellows, a bench, a quench barrel.

### The Shop Front
A bright room at the front of the cottage, with a counter, a bell, and a wide window that looks onto the lane. Sound carries well in here. The cottage's own shop can pass as a music stall from outside.
- *Read-aloud:* "The room is bright, with a counter, a bell, and a good echo. Even a whisper carries to the back wall."

### The three bedrooms
Three rooms along a corridor that is longer than the cottage should hold. Doors have blank nameplates that fill in when the owner chooses. Furnishings take the shape of whatever the sleeper describes. What is in each room is up to each player.
- *Read-aloud:* "The nameplate on the door is blank. When you touch it, the room beyond waits for you to decide what it should hold."
- **Ask each player:** what is on your wall? What did you bring? What is by the bed?

### The Conservatory
A glass room full of living plants, with a small round pond holding tiny glowing fish. A moss wall has a brass magnifier on a chain, so you can look at the tiny worlds living in the moss.
- *Read-aloud:* "The air is warm and green and wet. Small lights drift in the pond. A magnifier hangs by the moss wall, and under it a whole other world is going about its business."
- **Wonder:** this is where Katara's nature facts live: tardigrades, glowing fish, small things surviving big weather.

### The Attic Observatory
A round room under the roof with a window in the ceiling and a brass telescope. The telescope sometimes shows places other than the sky.
- *Read-aloud:* "A telescope sits under a round roof window. Tonight the stars look closer than they should."
- **Open:** where the telescope points when it points elsewhere is your call.

### The Bath
A copper tub warmed by a plate in the floor. The house heats the water when someone asks politely.

### The Cellar
Cool, dry stone, with barrels and a root store. The back door opens onto a small walled garden with an apple tree and a pond.

## The secret, the danger, the opportunity
- **Secret:** a door that is not on the plan appears behind the pantry on cloudy days. Where it leads is open.
- **Danger:** a door left open in a storm lets the cottage "inhale" a gust. Chaos, not harm.
- **Opportunity:** a hidden, comfortable base in the middle of Waterdeep.
