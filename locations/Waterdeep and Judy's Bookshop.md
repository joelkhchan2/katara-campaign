# Waterdeep and Judy's Bookshop
Judy's family runs a bookshop in Waterdeep. It is the party's base between adventures. Session 2 opened there: press came to interview the "two unlikely heroes," and Clarence arrived with his mission.
- **Proposed (not played):** Secret: the shelves hold a few books from the North Pole that no Waterdeep dealer should have. Danger: a customer who asks too many questions about the Star. Opportunity: a shop full of strange facts for a curious player.
