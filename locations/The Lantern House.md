# The Lantern House

**Status:** Proposed base (Session 3 option A). Not yet played. Everything below is a proposal until the table sees it.
**Where:** On the cliffs north of Waterdeep harbor, past the last of the fish stalls. Ninety steps to the top, forty more to climb from the road.
**One line:** An old lighthouse whose lamp has never quite gone out, with enough rooms inside its round walls to be a home and a workshop.

## From outside
A tall white tower with a black cap, streaked by weather and wound with salt-bitten ivy. The door is a deep green and has a brass knocker in the shape of a tiny lamp. Wind pulls at the gallery rail. Gulls complain about everything. Past the garden gate, a wall of wind-bent roses leans toward the sea, and in summer they are the only color for a mile.

## The magic of the place
- **The Keeper's Light.** The lamp in the top room burns with a steady white-gold glow that is not oil and not fire. It stays lit through storms. It keeps the whole tower warm and the drafts out. Anyone who lives here and steps outside can look back and see the glow brighten when someone is home and dim when the tower is empty.
- **Keeper's glass.** The bedroom windows are old, wavy panes. Whatever the weather, they show a calm night sky.
- **Warm by the lamp.** By day the great lens gathers sunlight. At night it releases the warmth through copper pipes that run down through the tower, heating the bath and the kitchen stove.
- **Moths.** Moths gather at the lamp room after dark. Nobody has explained a pattern in how they gather. This is the open clue for Session 3.

## Floor by floor
Read-aloud lines are short on purpose. Ask "what do you do?" after each.

### Cliff path and garden
Forty rough steps cut into rock lead up to a low stone wall and a rusted gate. A sign hangs from one hinge: "KEEPER WANTED. Apply within. Do not apply before breakfast."
- *Read-aloud:* "The gate shrieks when you push it. Salt wind, gull noise, and the smell of roses that should not be blooming this late. Above you, the tower's door is the only green thing on the cliff."

### Ground floor: the Keeper's Hall
A round room with a black iron stove, a copper kettle that is always almost boiling, and a long scarred table with initials carved in it from a hundred years of keepers. Hooks hold oilskin coats in sizes that do not match anyone. This is the shop counter if the table picks that upgrade, and the party's front room either way.
- *Read-aloud:* "The kitchen smells of lamp oil and old tea. Every surface is a little sticky and none of it is yours yet. There is a kettle on the stove, already warm."
- **Accommodations:** a stove, a pump with fresh water, a pantry with a tin of biscuits that is somehow always full. Coats for anyone who needs one.

### Oil cellar and forge (below ground)
A vaulted stone room where the keepers stored oil. Barrels are stacked like bricks along one wall. A flue runs up the wall and out through the gallery. This is the forge if the table picks that upgrade, and the best place in the tower for hot work.
- *Read-aloud:* "The steps go down and the air gets cooler and smells of stone and salt. The barrels along the wall are empty, but they still hum a little when the wind is right."
- **Accommodations:** a flue and bellows, a rain cistern, a long bench, and a door in the far wall that leads to the sea cave.

### Sea cave (below the cellar)
A tidal cave under the tower, with a small stone dock. At night the water glows a faint blue from living plankton. A tin lantern hangs from a hook, unlit.
- *Read-aloud:* "The cave breathes with the tide. Blue light drifts under the water like slow stars. A small boat sits tied to the dock, patched so many times it is mostly patch."
- **Danger:** the tide fills the dock twice a day. **Opportunity:** a private boat landing. **Open:** an old tin chest is wedged in a crack. What is in it is your call.

### Second floor: the Reading Room
Curved shelves wrap around the walls, following the stairwell as it climbs. A round table sits under a window seat. The books are mostly charts, logs, and tide tables, plus a few that smell of peppermint and pine. This is the reading-room upgrade if the table picks it.
- *Read-aloud:* "The shelves curve with the wall so every book leans a little toward you. A window seat faces the sea. A cup of cold tea is still on the table, as if someone had just stepped out."
- **Accommodations:** a table, a lamp that never needs lighting, and a ladder on a rail that slides around the room.

### Floors three to five: the bedrooms
Three wedge-shaped rooms, one per floor, each with a bed built into an alcove with a sliding panel, a small wardrobe, a stool, and a round window of keeper's glass. The rooms are plain. What is in them is up to each player.
- *Read-aloud:* "The room is a slice of the tower, curving in on one side. The window looks out on a calm night sky, though it is noon and there is a storm coming. The bed is a nook with a sliding door. There is nothing on the walls yet."
- **Ask each player:** what is on your wall? What did you bring? What do you leave on the stool?

### Floor six: the bath
A copper tub the size of a rowboat, heated by pipes from the lamp. A tall window shows the horizon.
- *Read-aloud:* "The pipes tick and groan, then the water starts to steam. The window is open a crack and the sea wind is exactly the right temperature."

### Floor seven: the Chart Room
A round table covered in charts of the coast. The coastlines redraw themselves slowly with the tide, so the table is never quite the same twice. A brass telescope points out of a window.
- *Read-aloud:* "The charts crawl, a thin line of ink at a time, as the tide goes out. The telescope is pointed at nothing in particular. Through it, a gull looks back at you."
- **Opportunity:** a place to track the Wonders on a map.

### The lamp room (top)
A great lens of nested glass rings, taller than a dwarf and wider than a table, surrounded by a ring of mirrors. At the center burns the Keeper's Light. The floor is wooden and creaks. A door leads out to the gallery. This is where the moths gather, and where the squatters nest in the squatters fight (Session 3, Beat 4).
- *Read-aloud:* "The light is white and gold and warm, and it fills the room without throwing a shadow. Dozens of moths wait in the air around it, very still, in neat rows."
- **Secret:** the keeper's logbook is on a shelf, with pages torn out. **Danger:** the gallery in a storm. **Opportunity:** the lens can focus light, which suits a Light cleric and a clever player with a mirror.
