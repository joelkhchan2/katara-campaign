# The Lantern House
The party's base of operations as of Session 3. An old lighthouse on the Waterdeep cliffs, long unused. (The alternative considered was a tiny cottage that is bigger inside.)

- **Sound and smell:** gulls, wind in the gallery rail, salt, old lamp oil, dust.
- **Layout:** a ground-floor keeper's room with a stove and a counter, a stairwell of 90 steps, a lamp room at the top with a big lens and a ring of mirrors, and a gallery outside.
- **Moths:** moths gather at the lamp room at night. Nobody has explained a pattern in how they gather. See `sessions/Session 3 Prep.md`.
- **Upgrades (Session 3):** a forge, a reading room and a shop counter, picked by the table.
- **Proposed (not played):** Secret: the keeper's logbook has pages torn out. Danger: the stairs and the gallery in a storm. Opportunity: the lens can focus light, which suits a Light cleric.
