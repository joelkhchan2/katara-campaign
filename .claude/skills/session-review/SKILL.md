# Reviewing a session prep doc

Run this as a deliberate pass, not from memory. The value is in checking each item against the doc rather than eyeballing it.

## How to run it
1. **Read the full prep doc top to bottom.** Stale content hides in parts nobody has touched lately.
2. **Walk the checklist below** and note gaps as you find them.
3. **Report gaps as a short list**, then fix mechanical ones (stale pointers, missing cross-references) directly. Anything that is new creative content goes back through the discuss-before-write rule in `CLAUDE.md`. Do not silently invent material to fill a gap.
4. **Give the "we're set" verdict only after the checklist has been walked.**

## The checklist

**Structure and cross-references**
- Every pointer to another doc or earlier section still resolves.
- The "Where things stand" recap at the top matches the doc's final content and the latest session record. Sessions are weeks or months apart, so this recap has to stand on its own.
- A "Previously" read-aloud recap exists for the start of the session.

**Mechanics**
- Every check has a DC where one is implied. Default DC ladder: 10 easy, 13 moderate, 15 hard, 18 very hard.
- Every creature has a stat block or a pointer to one. Balanced for three level-3 PCs.
- Loot, prices and gp totals are internally consistent.
- Level-ups since last session are reflected (subclass choices, new spells, new features).

**Spotlight and tone**
- Judy, Dormir and Lyric each get a real, active beat, not a mention. Check for one PC having all the active material.
- Judy's beat is a good fit for a newer player: a clue to find, a choice to make, a moment where she can lead.
- The tone matches the compass in `CLAUDE.md` (about 50% mystery, 30% comedy, 20% wonder, PG) and counterweights how the last session ran.
- Any antagonist or obstacle has an identity and a motive, not just a stat line.

**Mystery and clues**
- Every mystery has a clue trail with redundancy: at least two ways to reach each key fact.
- Clues are concrete, fair and plantable. Nothing the players must know is locked behind one failed roll.
- Katara can solve it herself with what is on the table.

**Plot and continuity**
- The session advances the Six Wonders spine, or is a deliberate bottle episode. Confirm which.
- New threads the session creates are banked in a "Where it goes next" section.
- Nothing contradicts settled canon. Spot-check names, numbers and facts against `campaign/Name Index.md` and the previous session record.

**Props and handouts**
- Every player-facing handout has been checked against the `handout-prop-split` skill.
- Props Katara will hold are readable for a sharp 13-year-old.

**Voice**
- Run the `dnd-writing` self-check on any new prose.

## What "set" means
State the verdict plainly: what is confirmed complete, what is open by design (a branch to resolve live at the table, not a gap), and what still needs a fix. Do not pad a clean result with hedging, and do not give a clean verdict without having walked the list.
